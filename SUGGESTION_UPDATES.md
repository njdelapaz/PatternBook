# AI Suggestions - Updated for Contextual Relevance

## Changes Made

The AI suggestions have been updated to be more directly relevant to the demo notes.

### New Art Suggestion (First Note - Dream Library)

**Previous**: Turtle in a Lotus Pond
**New**: Giuseppe Arcimboldo's "The Librarian" (1526)

**Why it's better:**
- The first demo note is about a dream of wandering through an endless library where books contain altered memories
- Arcimboldo's painting depicts a human portrait composed entirely of books
- This surreal fusion of person and library directly mirrors the dream's theme of identity, memory, and stories being inseparable
- Much more thematically relevant than a turtle in a pond

**Connection to the note:**
> "I had the most interesting dream last night about wandering through an endless library. Each book seemed to contain memories from my life..."

The artwork shows a figure that IS the library—books forming a human, just as the dream explores how memories and stories form our identity.

### New Quote Suggestion (Second Note - Productivity)

**Previous**: Winston Churchill - "If you're going through hell, keep going."
**New**: Henry David Thoreau - "It is not enough to be busy. So are the ants. The question is: What are we busy about?"

**Why it's better:**
- The second demo note is about realizing that productivity isn't about accomplishing more, but being intentional
- Churchill's quote is about perseverance through hardship (not relevant to the note)
- Thoreau's quote directly challenges busyness for its own sake
- Perfectly addresses the note's core insight about quality over quantity

**Connection to the note:**
> "I've been measuring my worth by how much I accomplish, but that's not sustainable. Maybe the goal isn't to do more, but to be more intentional about what I choose to do."

Thoreau's question "What are we busy about?" cuts directly to the heart of this realization.
